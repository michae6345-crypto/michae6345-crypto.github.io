Project · 2026 · TrojanHacks

Monte

Rewards apps show you points. Monte shows what your credit cards really cost you: a year of rewards and perks used, minus annual fees and interest, per card. It replays the same purchases on the best card already in your wallet, ranks your cards at the register, and says which cards are worth keeping.

I built the backend: the Plaid integration, a Monte Carlo simulation that resamples your own months into 1,000 simulated years, a linear-programming optimizer that checks greedy card routing against the true optimum under spending caps, and the test suite. The AI never does the math; every dollar traces to a published card rule.

Built at ACM@USC TrojanHacks 2026, Finance track.
