Benchmarking PINNs Across Precision and Optimizers

Benchmarked physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) across eight precision and optimizer configurations on four benchmark PDEs (Allen-Cahn, wave, reaction, Burgers). Found that precision matters far more under L-BFGS than under Adam, and that every low-error run finished in L-BFGS. Accepted to the NeurIPS OPT workshop.
