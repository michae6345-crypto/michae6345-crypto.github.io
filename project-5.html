← back to projects compllm · 2026

compllm is a stylometric attribution engine i built to identify which llm generated a given piece of text, based on stylistic fingerprints rather than watermarks or metadata. the core challenge was finding features that actually separate models reliably instead of just picking up on topic or length. it turned into a good testbed for thinking about how distinguishable model outputs really are once you strip away the obvious tells.
